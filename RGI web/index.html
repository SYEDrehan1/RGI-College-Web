Institutional Overview

Dedicated to Innovative and Holistic Education

Fostering a student-centered ecosystem where academic learning converges with practical capability and personal growth.

Established in 2015 in Bengaluru, Rajadhani Group of Institution has earned a strong reputation for academic rigor and student-centric pedagogy. We believe that true learning requires a balance between theoretical depth, practical execution, and personal mentorship.

Our academic model integrates robust teaching methodologies, continuous skill development, rigorous laboratory practice, and active mentorship. We emphasize all-round personality development so that every graduate emerges resilient, articulate, and industry-ready.

Institutional Philosophy: "Mission Possible"

At Rajadhani, we operate on the fundamental belief that "Every Problem Has a Solution." This mindset encourages our students to view complex academic and real-world challenges not as obstacles, but as opportunities for innovation and growth.

Official Admission Guidelines

1. Application & Registration

Candidates seeking admission to PU or Degree courses must complete the prescribed application form either through the online portal or at the college admissions office.

2. Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • Original SSLC / 10th Standard Marks Card (for PU Admission)
  • Original PUC / 10+2 / Equivalent Marks Card (for Degree Admission)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous institution
  • Conduct Certificate from the head of the institution last attended
  • Migration Certificate (for non-Karnataka / ICSE / CBSE students)
  • Recent passport-size photographs (5 copies)
  • Aadhaar Card copy of candidate and parent

3. Counseling & Seat Allocation

Seats are allocated based on merit and availability. Candidates and their parents will participate in an academic counseling session to finalize elective combinations.

Privacy Policy
